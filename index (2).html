An extra safety check before imports

CatalogGuard is an experimental offline toolkit for operators who maintain product catalogs. The current prototype is a limited checker, not a full Shopify import validator.

01 / PREPARE

Use your CSV export

Inspect a product CSV before making changes to your live Shopify catalog.

02 / REVIEW

See risky rows

Spot common data issues and focus your review on the records that need attention.

03 / DECIDE

Import with care

Use the findings alongside backups and a small test import; no tool guarantees a safe import.
